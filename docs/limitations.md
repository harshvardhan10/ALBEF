# Limitations and scientific claims

The September design, reconfirmed by the author, states that localization conclusions are qualitative. ALBEF heatmaps did not support a meaningful final quantitative localization ranking. FROC utilities remain exploratory. Quantitative classification uses validation-selected checkpoint/threshold roles and must be reported separately.

Historical observations in the design say some FC/mean pleural-effusion cases looked better aligned while more complex fusion could be diffuse, central or off-target; the original single-view model remained competitive. These are author-provided historical observations, not newly measured results. Cross-attention findings remain pending even though training is complete.

No new semi-supervised method, bone-suppression method, improvement in weak localization, or full training equivalence is asserted by cleanup. All-zero maps and poor overlays are evidence to report rather than hide. Local CPU tests lack the server checkpoints, original environment, patient data and GPU. Baseline/refactored server comparison is required before altering the fusion models.
